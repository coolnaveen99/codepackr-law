# Legal Content Methodology

## Source hierarchy

Prefer authoritative primary sources for current Indian law. Where secondary explanations are used, keep them clearly distinguishable from the law itself.

## Versioning

Every important legal topic should preserve the applicable Act, amendment, notification or case-law date context. Avoid presenting historical wording as current law.

## Student-friendly structure

A strong topic should normally contain:

1. Meaning and introduction
2. Historical or legislative background where relevant
3. Essential ingredients
4. Explanation of each ingredient
5. Exceptions and limitations
6. Illustrations or examples
7. Leading case laws
8. Critical analysis
9. Exam-oriented conclusion
10. Related doctrines and cross-references
