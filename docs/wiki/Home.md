## Core principles

1. Indian law content must identify the relevant law and version/date context.
2. Primary sources take priority for statutes, rules, notifications and case law.
3. Never fabricate a section, case, quotation or legal proposition.
4. Study content should be textbook-style and useful for 10-mark and 16-mark answers.
5. Reusable legal concepts, doctrines and cases should have stable tags so they can be referenced rather than duplicated.
6. Educational content is not a substitute for professional legal advice.
