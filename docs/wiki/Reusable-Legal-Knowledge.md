# Reusable Legal Knowledge

Legal knowledge should be stored as reusable concepts instead of duplicated paragraphs.

## Recommended tags

- Doctrine
- Legal Principle
- Case Law
- Statutory Provision
- Definition
- Exception
- Illustration
- Historical Note

## Reuse rule

When the same doctrine or case is required in multiple subjects, reference the canonical tagged entry and add topic-specific explanation around it. Do not create multiple conflicting copies.

The canonical entry should record its source, legal status, relevant jurisdiction and update date.
