# AI and Content Rules

AI-assisted content must remain source-grounded.

## Required checks

- Verify statutory sections against authoritative sources.
- Verify case names, citations and propositions.
- Do not invent quotations.
- Preserve amendment and repeal status.
- Separate black-letter law from commentary and interpretation.
- Do not turn generated educational material into individualized legal advice.

AI agents should read the project instructions, skills and reusable-knowledge rules before creating or modifying legal content.
