# Trust-Preserving Monetization — Codepackr Law

**Phase:** 32  
**Product:** law.codepackr.com  
**Date:** 2026-09-30  
**Status:** Binding product policy

## Principle

The **basic legal-information and productivity layer remains free**.

Monetization must not undermine trust, privacy, or access to educational legal tools.

## Free layer (must stay free)

- Curriculum subjects and topic study surfaces  
- Bare-act / Sanhita educational mappers and transition highlights  
- MCQ / practice / exam educational modes  
- Citation and judgment educational utilities (client-side)  
- Research / case-prep / draft educational tools (browser-local)  
- Primary source finder and official-link directory  
- Privacy controls and local-only storage model  
- Basic legal safety and educational disclaimers  

Do **not** put basic legal safety information or core educational library access behind a paywall.

## Optional future paid features (not implemented)

These are allowed as **optional** future products only after privacy and trust review:

- larger workspace quotas;  
- optional cloud sync (opt-in, never default for case facts);  
- advanced export packs;  
- team / chamber workspace;  
- firm integrations;  
- institution dashboards;  
- licensed commercial datasets (separate from free educational layer).  

None of the above are required for Phases 0–32 completion.

## Advertising rules

- Do not place **aggressive** advertising inside sensitive legal-document workflows (e.g. draft studio, case prep, document compare while the user is editing privileged text).  
- Site-level ads (if any) must not imply endorsement of legal advice or court outcomes.  
- Ads must not collect case facts or draft content.  

## Trust commitments

| Commitment | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Free core | Study + educational tools stay available without payment |
| Privacy-first | Default remains browser-local; cloud is opt-in only if ever added |
| No outcome sales | Never sell “win probability” or judicial prediction |
| No paywalled safety | Disclaimers, source links, and basic educational access stay free |
| Transparent tiers | Any future paid feature must be labelled optional |
