# CodePackr Law — AI Prompts Library

This folder contains standardized prompts to instruct AI assistants (Claude, ChatGPT, Copilot, DeepSeek, Cursor, etc.) to generate or update content according to **Senior Counsel & PhD Standards**.

## Available Prompts

1. **[`TOPIC_AUTHORING_AI_PROMPT.md`](./TOPIC_AUTHORING_AI_PROMPT.md)**
   - **Purpose:** Full treatise generation for any provision or legal topic in `src/data/topics/<subject>/<topicId>.ts`.
   - **Features:** 5 doctrinal modules, dual statutory illustrations (applies vs fails), extracted case ratios (`ratioDecidendi`, `court`, `bench`), chamber IRAC problem assessment, and appellate written submissions. Zero exam marks.
